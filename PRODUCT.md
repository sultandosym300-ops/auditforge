# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by user: React + Python 3.11. User chose code-first implementation based on apple.mp4.
## Users
Hackathon participants creating and evaluating reproducible financial-audit RL environments; judges inspecting correctness.
## Product Purpose
AuditForge generates fictional companies, exposes bounded investigation tools and evaluates evidence-backed findings reproducibly.
## Operating Context
Offline CPU-only official evaluation. Public records separated from private truth and provenance. UI presents environment operation; it does not claim model improvement.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Four independently configurable issue types, two interactions, clean distractors, reset/step, 100-step budget, safe calculator, exact matcher, four policies, trajectories, 10 train and 10 dev episodes. Landing then mandatory account/login/guest choice, top workspace tabs, mini tutorial, Russian/Kazakh/English.
## Brand Commitments
The user's apple.mp4 is binding visual evidence: SF-style typography, large product scenes, generous light areas alternating with dark cinematic scenes, restrained navigation, meaningful cursor-responsive 3D and smooth scroll animation.
## Evidence on Hand
Original case and extended mission at the two supplied attachment paths. No official hidden tests or judging result available.
## Product Principles
Evidence before claims. Reproducible calculations. Clear first action. Presentation motion does not obstruct work.
